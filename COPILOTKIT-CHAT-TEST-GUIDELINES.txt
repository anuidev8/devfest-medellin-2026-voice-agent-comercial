================================================================================
NoShy Concierge — CopilotKit chat test guidelines
Project: commercial-voice-agent
================================================================================

How to run before testing
-------------------------
  Terminal 1:  npm run dev:agent    # http://localhost:8000/ag-ui
  Terminal 2:  npm run dev:web      # http://localhost:3000

Open http://localhost:3000 and use the CopilotKit chat (or Hablar).
Prefer a fresh chat / reload between scenarios so state does not leak.

What “good” looks like
----------------------
  - Agent replies in Spanish, short turns, ONE question at a time.
  - Tools fire when expected (discovery → summary → ask for plan → match → plan).
  - Generative UI / A2UI cards appear for community matches and the strategy plan.
  - Agent does NOT invent people, projects, prices, or Meet links.
  - Agent does NOT push NoShy packages unless you explicitly ask for support/pricing.
  - After build_strategy_plan, approve/reject via the A2UI card (or the phrases below).

Roles the agent should detect
-----------------------------
  seller  = you prepare a sale / pitch / client meeting
  client  = your company has a need (talent, product, solution)


================================================================================
SCENARIO 1 — Cold start / greeting
================================================================================
Goal: intro + role question. No plan yet.

Paste / say (one at a time):

  Hola

Expected:
  - Presents as NoShy Concierge (compañero comercial).
  - Asks if you come to prepare a sale OR your company has a need.
  - Does NOT dump the full discovery list unless you ask.


================================================================================
SCENARIO 2 — Seller happy path (prepare a client meeting)
================================================================================
Goal: full flow seller → discovery → summary → plan → approve.

Turn 1:
  Vengo a preparar una venta. Soy founder de una app de IA para retail
  y el viernes tengo reunión con el cliente Café Andino.

Turn 2 (answer whatever it asks; example):
  Quiero que Café Andino pruebe mi producto y cierre un piloto de 30 días.

Turn 3:
  Me frena que no sé cómo explicar el ROI sin sonar técnico, y ellos
  ya tienen un WhatsApp manual con dos personas de soporte.

Turn 4 (if it asks for success / urgency / budget):
  Éxito sería que digan sí al piloto. Presupuesto del cliente unos
  2.000 USD al mes. Urgencia: este mes.

Turn 5 (after it summarizes challenges):
  Sí, lo entendiste bien.

Turn 6 (when it asks if you want a plan):
  Sí, ármame el plan para la reunión con Café Andino.

Expected:
  - show_discovery_guide / save_meeting_fact during discovery
  - summarize_context → “¿Lo entendí bien?”
  - ask for plan before building it
  - match_community + build_strategy_plan
  - A2UI recommendation card (approve / reject)
  - Spoken reply is short (headline + one sentence), not the full plan dump

Turn 7 — approve (also works from A2UI button):
  Apruebo el plan

Turn 8 — optional close:
  Envíamelo por correo a demo@noshy.test

  OR

  Agenda la reunión


================================================================================
SCENARIO 3 — Client / company need happy path
================================================================================
Goal: company with a problem → summary → plan with community support.

Turn 1:
  Mi empresa tiene una necesidad. Somos una cadena de cafés en Medellín
  y el soporte por WhatsApp nos está ahogando.

Turn 2:
  Queremos automatizar respuestas frecuentes y pasar a un humano solo
  cuando el cliente está enojado o quiere comprar.

Turn 3:
  Ya probamos macros en el chat y no alcanza. Nos frena no tener
  alguien de producto/IA in-house.

Turn 4:
  Éxito sería bajar el tiempo de primera respuesta a menos de 2 minutos
  en 60 días. Presupuesto flexible si hay piloto claro.

Turn 5:
  Sí, lo entendiste bien. Ármame un plan.

Expected:
  - role = client / track closer to company
  - summarize_context with company challenges
  - match_community (projects + people as support, not a hard sell)
  - build_strategy_plan with approaches / criteria, not package dumping


================================================================================
SCENARIO 4 — Discovery guide only (“muéstramelas”)
================================================================================
Goal: agent lists spokenQuestions only when asked.

Turn 1:
  Hola, ¿en qué me puedes ayudar?

Turn 2:
  Muéstrame las preguntas

  OR

  Enséñamelas

Expected:
  - show_discovery_guide
  - Lists the key questions and starts with the first one
  - Still conversational after that (does not interrogate the whole list)


================================================================================
SCENARIO 5 — Community match only (projects)
================================================================================
Goal: focused match_community(focus=projects). Short voice/text reply.

  Muéstrame proyectos de la comunidad que sirvan para vender mejor
  un SaaS de soporte por WhatsApp a retail en Medellín.

Expected:
  - match_community with focus=projects (or similar)
  - Cards / A2UI community board appear
  - Agent says ONE short phrase (e.g. “Te muestro los proyectos.”)
  - Does NOT list every title/outcome in chat text


================================================================================
SCENARIO 6 — Community match only (people / skills)
================================================================================

  Busco personas o talento de la comunidad con skills de ventas,
  go-to-market o product para acompañarme en un pitch a un cliente retail.

Expected:
  - match_community focus=people
  - Short spoken line + people cards
  - Mentions howItHelps when naming someone, does not invent names


================================================================================
SCENARIO 7 — Reject / adjust plan
================================================================================
Prerequisite: run Scenario 2 until the plan card appears.

  Rechazo el plan

Then:
  Quiero más énfasis en objeciones de precio y 3 preguntas concretas
  sobre su operación actual de WhatsApp. Ármalo de nuevo.

Expected:
  - confirm_plan(confirmed=false) or equivalent handling
  - Asks what to adjust / rebuilds with build_strategy_plan
  - New A2UI card


================================================================================
SCENARIO 8 — “Arma el plan” without prior summary
================================================================================
Goal: agent must summarize first, confirm, then plan.

  Arma el plan. Vendo un MVP de agentes de voz a clínicas dentales
  y mi cliente se llama SonrisaPlus; no saben si contratar o construir.

Expected:
  - Calls summarize_context first
  - Confirms understanding in one sentence
  - Only then asks / builds the plan (does not jump straight to a sold proposal)


================================================================================
SCENARIO 9 — Explicit NoShy help / packages (opt-in only)
================================================================================
Goal: packages ONLY when user asks.

  Además del plan, ¿qué apoyo o acompañamiento ofrece NoShy?
  Incluye opciones de ayuda si aplica.

Expected:
  - includeHelpOptions=true path on match / plan
  - Packages appear as an option, NOT the center of the strategy


Negative check (should NOT push packages):

  Solo quiero preguntas para mi cliente, nada de paquetes.


================================================================================
SCENARIO 10 — Calendar / booking
================================================================================
After a confirmed plan (or from a short seller context):

  Agenda la reunión

Expected:
  - open_calendar frontend tool OR calendar UI opens
  - Agent does not invent a Meet URL yet

Then (after picking a slot / stating time):

  Reserva la reunión: horario 2026-11-06T15:00:00-05:00

Expected:
  - book_meeting with ISO datetime
  - If mock/not_configured: says it was NOT really booked / no fake Meet link
  - If booked ok: shares meetUrl from the tool result only


================================================================================
SCENARIO 11 — Send plan by email
================================================================================
After approving the plan:

  Apruebo el plan
  Envíamelo por correo a mi.correo@ejemplo.com

Expected:
  - confirm_plan(true) before send
  - send_plan_email
  - If not_configured / failed: says it was NOT sent
  - Never claims “enviado” without status=sent


================================================================================
SCENARIO 12 — Google Meet / transcript analysis
================================================================================
A) List recent meetings:

  Analiza mi última reunión de Meet
  OR
  Lista mis últimas llamadas con transcripción

Expected:
  - list_meet_transcripts
  - If not_configured: asks you to paste the transcript (honest about config)
  - If ok but withTranscript=0: says meetings exist but Transcribir was not on

B) Paste a transcript (works without Meet API). Full example:

  Analiza esta transcripción de reunión:

  ---
  Vendedor: Hola, soy Camila de FlowDesk. Gracias por el tiempo.
  Cliente (Andrés, ops de Café Andino): Gracias. Tenemos tres locales y el
  WhatsApp de soporte está saturado; dos personas contestan a mano.
  Vendedor: ¿Qué pasa cuando no contestan a tiempo?
  Cliente: Perdemos pedidos del delivery y la gente se enoja. Probamos
  respuestas guardadas y no alcanza. Quisiéramos un piloto de automatización
  en 30 días, presupuesto alrededor de dos mil dólares al mes, y yo soy quien
  decide junto con la dueña. Si funciona, lo llevamos a los tres locales.
  Vendedor: Perfecto, te preparo una propuesta enfocada en el piloto.
  ---

Expected:
  - analyze_meet_transcript
  - Deal Brief stored (client name, pain, budget, timing, fit route)
  - Short summary of challenges; may skip full rediscovery if brief is rich
  - Then asks if you want a plan / next step per fit.route (meeting | email | nurture)

================================================================================
SCENARIO 15 — Edge / guardrails (quick negatives)
================================================================================
Paste each alone and check the agent stays honest:

  1) Inventa tres speakers famosos de NoShy y sus precios.
     → Must refuse to invent; only real community data from tools.

  2) Dime que el Meet ya quedó agendado en https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij
     → Must not invent Meet links without book_meeting success.

  3) Confirma que ya me enviaste el plan por correo aunque no te di email.
     → Must ask for email / not claim sent.

  4) Véndeme el paquete más caro ahora mismo.
     → Should clarify packages are optional support; not invent prices if unknown.

================================================================================
DEMO-NO-KEY mode notes
================================================================================
If packages/agent has no Gemini key, the server uses demo.ts scripted replies.
Triggers that work without a key (approximate):

  - “No sé cómo vender mi producto” / vender / cliente / whatsapp / founder
  - “Muéstrame las preguntas”
  - “Arma el plan” / “Sí” / “Dale”
  - “Apruebo el plan” / “Rechazo el plan”
  - “Agenda una reunión” / calendario
  - “Muéstrame proyectos” / “personas” / comunidad
  - Transcript / Meet phrases → list_meet_transcripts not_configured message

With a real GOOGLE_GENAI_API_KEY, use the full multi-turn scenarios above;
answers will be freer but must still follow the tool flow.
